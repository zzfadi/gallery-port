A local personal workflow tool

CoFlow

CoFlow is Fadi Zuabi’s locally operated assistant for managing conversations, projects, and case workflows. Each configured group has its own access and processing policy. Connecting a group does not automatically enable AI processing.

Privacy notice

Updated October 10, 2026. This notice describes CoFlow’s local runtime and its optional Google integrations.

Gmail access

CoFlow requests only Gmail’s send-email permission to deliver operational failure alerts to the account owner’s designated address. It does not request permission to read, search, or modify the mailbox. Alerts contain a generic operational notice and a local alert reference, without conversation contents or case details.

The OAuth client and refresh credential are stored in macOS Keychain on the operator’s Mac. Google processes the email and its delivery metadata when an alert is sent. CoFlow records the alert’s delivery status and message identifier locally.

Conversation and AI processing

Selected WhatsApp conversations and delivery records are stored locally, outside the operator’s Google Drive synchronization folders. Manual case workflows remain local and do not send their contents to Gemini. When the operator explicitly enables Gemini for a project, the inputs needed for that project are sent to Google’s Gemini API under the applicable Google service terms and data policies. Audio and file contents require separate handling policies; they are not automatically uploaded to an AI service.

CoFlow does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send Gmail data to AI models for training or inference. CoFlow’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Local conversation records, delivery history, and operational evidence remain until the operator deletes them. Temporary media follows its configured expiry. Deleting a local record does not delete copies held by recipients or service providers.

The account owner can disconnect Google access through Google Account connections, stop CoFlow locally, and remove its credentials from macOS Keychain. Local data can be deleted from the CoFlow application-support directory. Access to a workflow can be revoked or changed separately for each group.
